Our Story

Brew & Bean was born out of a simple love for a great cup of coffee. What started as a small home-roasting experiment in 2020 has grown into a thriving community hub in the heart of Cape Town.

We believe that coffee is more than just a morning routine—it's a connection to the farmers who grow it, the roasters who craft it, and the people we share it with.

Our Values

Quality First

We never compromise on the quality of our beans. Every batch is cupped and tested to ensure it meets our high standards.

Community Focused

Our shop is a space for everyone. Whether you're a student studying for exams, a freelancer working remotely, or a friend catching up, you are welcome here.

Environmental Responsibility

We are committed to reducing our waste and using compostable packaging wherever possible.
